# Corrections

## 2026-10-27 — errata to z20-equals-6 candidate-2026-07-26 (DOI 10.5281/zenodo.21647645)

The archived PDF, the manuscript source, the Zenodo deposit and the tag `candidate-2026-07-26` are unchanged. These errata were confirmed by the Evidence Press archive audit and are also shown at <https://evidencepress.org/releases/z20-equals-6/>.

1. Corrected a description in the last paragraph of §6.1. The archived text says that “the second admissibility condition says that G[V \ S] is triangle-free, while the absence of a K4 in the core says that this complement has independence number at most three”. The admissibility condition is that G[V \ S] contains no independent triple, so the sentence should read: “the second admissibility condition says that the complement of G[V \ S] is triangle-free, while the absence of a K4 in G says that this complement has independence number at most three”.

   Only the explanatory sentence is affected; the argument it summarises uses the correct condition. No theorem, certificate, computation or stated result is affected.

## 2026-10-27 — errata to vr2-k4-equals-20 0.1-candidate (DOI 10.5281/zenodo.21647654)

The archived PDF, the manuscript source, the Zenodo deposit and the tag `vr2-k4-v0.1-candidate` are unchanged. These errata were confirmed by the Evidence Press archive audit and are also shown at <https://evidencepress.org/releases/vr2-k4-equals-20/>.

1. Corrected a word in the proof of Lemma 3.2. The archived text says “the edges of P17[M] form the eight-cycle 2−12−15−4−16−6−3−14−2 together with 2 16, 12 6, 15 3, 4 14”. These twelve pairs are the non-edges of P17[M], as the later remark on its 12 non-edges says. The proof should read: “The non-edges of P17[M] form the eight-cycle … This graph is triangle-free. Therefore P17[M] has no independent triple.”

   The conclusion was rechecked by computation (P17[M] has no independent triple). No theorem, certificate, computation or stated result is affected.
